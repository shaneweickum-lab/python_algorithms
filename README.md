## python_algorithms
#Purpose
This repository documents my independent study of computer science, machine learning, neural networks, and artificial intelligence from first principles. Implementations are written by me as learning exercises rather than generated as finished solutions by AI. AI may be used as a tutor to explain concepts, review my reasoning, and help me debug after I have attempted a solution.
The objective is not to avoid AI-assisted engineering. I use AI-assisted development elsewhere. The objective here is different: to develop and demonstrate my own understanding of the algorithms, mathematics, and architectures underneath those tools.
